# Exam Preparation Strategy (synthetic study skills guide)

## Spaced revision
Spread revision over several days instead of one long session. Revisit a topic after one day, three days and one week to strengthen recall.

## Active recall
Testing yourself with practice questions beats rereading. Prioritise topics where your recent quiz accuracy is lowest.

## Planning rules
Schedule weaker topics earlier and more often. Keep sessions between 30 and 60 minutes with a short break. Reserve the final two days before an exam for mixed practice and light review, not new material.

## Replanning
If the exam date moves or a topic score changes sharply, rebuild the remaining plan and keep sessions that are already completed.
